My Story

My life experiences have shaped the person I am and the future I want to create. Growing up, I wished for more financial stability, better opportunities, and the kind of love and support that could make life feel different. Those are things I carry with me, not because I want to live in the past, but because I want to build something better for my future.

I'm a quiet person who listens and observes more than I talk. Sometimes people see how I act without understanding the experiences behind it. There's more to me than what people see on the surface, and my story is bigger than the assumptions people make about me.

My Dreams

I'm pursuing Information Technology with the goal of becoming an AI engineer. I also express myself through music as YGD3x and have a creative vision for my clothing brand, BTRT (Broke Today Rich Tomorrow).

Whether my future takes me through technology, music, entrepreneurship, or a combination of all three, I want to keep growing and creating opportunities for myself.

The Future I Want

More than anything, I want happiness. I want genuine friendships, a place where I feel at home, and a loving family of my own. I want my future children to grow up feeling supported, valued, and loved. I want to give them the kind of stability and love I wished for growing up.

My goal isn't just to become successful. It's to build a life that makes me happy and a family that never has to question whether they're loved.
